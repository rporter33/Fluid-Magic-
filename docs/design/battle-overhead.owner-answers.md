# Owner answers, 2026-10-23 (binding; quote verbatim in docs)

After the overhead battle mockup (https://claude.ai/artifact/9N3xAjDepZNfKB4KnrPs4D), which replays three real sim
battles with procedural placeholder figures, the owner wrote:

> The painted unit art is what would make this stand out, on top of additional animations, and the salamander
> portrait doesn't match the salamander in game. But the formations and effects so far are great. This is a good
> direction, increasing the fidelity, and the detail is what we're looking to achieve here. Cities should also be
> included in a later build.

Read as:

1. **Approved:** the overhead stage of `battle-overhead.md`: the camera, formations by kind, monsters at true size,
   volleys, blood and bodies, the card bar and the hover card ("the formations and effects so far are great", "a good
   direction").
2. **Next:** painted unit art for every figure on the field, in the painted style of the approved leader portraits, and
   more animation (attacks, hits, deaths, marching, galloping, drawing and loosing, monster attacks, routs). Fidelity
   and detail are the goal.
3. **A unit on the field and its portrait must match.** The Salamander's card portrait (an approved leader portrait)
   and its figure on the field look like different creatures; the figure is made to match the portrait, and the same
   holds for every commander, summon and monster that has a portrait.
4. **Later build:** cities in battles. A battle fought where a town or city stands shows it, and a storm shows the
   city behind its walls. Scenery still never suggests cover or a chokepoint the rules do not model.

## 2026-10-24, the spell-effects spec from `claude/m1-design-docs` against `docs/12`

Another session's commit 8056fa7 (on `claude/m1-design-docs`) gave `docs/09` effect families with a 900 ms cap and
12 to 40 particles, and said units never glow. `docs/12` S2.10, written earlier at the owner's direction, makes
Fireball last 1.9 s and Entangle 1.4 s with 40 to 160 particles, and the art plan lets creatures glow while using
their fire. Asked which should win, the owner chose:

> Keep the flashier timing (Recommended)

The option as offered: "Fireball 1.9 s, Entangle 1.4 s, the particle counts you asked for. Adopt everything else
from the new spec: effect families, colour styles, at most 3 big effects at once, numbers on top, the gallery,
shorter versions at 2x speed and up."

> Glow only during own fire (Recommended)

The option as offered: "No unit glows at rest. A creature's cracks or veins light up only while it uses its own fire
ability, as part of that effect. Spell light may also tint nearby figures."

Read as: `docs/12`'s beats, durations and particle counts stand for the eleven M1 battle spells; the families, the
color grammar, the glow container rules, the readability rules, the three-effect cap, numbers above effects,
`content/fx.ts`, the effects gallery and the 2x short form are adopted (`docs/02`, `06`, `09`, `10` carry both); a
unit never glows at rest, and a creature's emissive parts light only during its own fire ability.
