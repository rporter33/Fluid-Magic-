Art prompts for ChatGPT

These are the images that replace the game's drawn-in-code placeholders: the oak table top, the tavern floor around it, and leader portraits. Nothing in the game waits for them, so make them whenever it suits you.

  1. Copy a prompt with its button and paste it into ChatGPT as written. Ask for the size shown on its card.
  2. Regenerate until it passes the "Looks right when" line on the card.
  3. Attach the image in our chat and say its code (for example "A1"). I check it, crop it, and add it to the game with a record of the prompt and the date.
