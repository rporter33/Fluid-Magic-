# Owner answers, 2026-10-23 (binding; quote verbatim in docs)

Asked the nine questions of `mid-game.md` section 12, the owner wrote:

> 1 3

> 6 three schools two levels each with a choice at every levels sounds immersive enough

Read as:

1. Seasons are three months each.
2. Supply reach before the penalty is **two hops** from the nearest own fort (the default said
   three). Each hop past two costs 20% of the supply the army finds, to the 20% floor.
3. Rivers protect defenders: +1 def for that battle when the attacker crossed a river.
4. Great Working slots: `floor(nations / 2) + 1`.
5. The Ascension hold period starts at six months and is tested; the runner and the long
   session decide whether it moves.
6. Research is three schools per color, two levels each, with a choice at every level.
7. Mercenaries come once M2 adds forts, for the long session.
8. The Vast map is **not capped at 200**: it reaches 270 provinces at eight nations and is
   measured (generation time, the tick budget, the overview band at 60 fps) before a cap is set.
9. Automation is available on every difficulty.

Two answers differ from the defaults (2 and 8); the record's sections 3 and 9 are updated to
them.
