Top left: dark ripples spreading on the ground: four concentric rings of shadow, inky and uneven, the inner ones darker, around a dark middle; a flat ellipse.
Top middle: inward tendrils: nine thin curling tendrils of shadow creeping inward over the ground to a dark middle, like ink drawn into a drain; a flat ellipse.
Top right: four drips of shadow, each a thin inky trickle running down to a heavy drop at its lower end.
Middle band: a long band of dark ripples crossing the whole width, two or three wavy inky bands with soft edges, for a ripple that rolls across the field.
Bottom two strips: two thin dark threads, inky and slightly twisting, the same thickness from end to end, each filling its strip, for a thread of drained life.
Soft, painterly ink and smoke edges; no hard vector lines.

Camera: the game's battle camera, high above the field and looking down at about 40 degrees, the way a player looks down at miniatures on a table; orthographic, no perspective. Anything lying on the ground is foreshortened: a round shape on the ground appears as a flat ellipse about two thirds as tall as it is wide.

These are flat marks on the ground (decals) that the game multiplies onto the battle field: pure white means untouched ground and every darker tone darkens the ground under it. Paint only the marks, as stains seen from above, with no light and shade of their own, no highlights brighter than the white, no cast shadows, no objects standing up.
Umbra's colours: deep violet-black ink (#1A1024) in the darkest strokes, a dusky plum-grey (#3D2B4F) body and soft grey edges that fade into the white; no bright purple, no other colour.

Painting style: painterly oil on a wood panel, visible directional brushwork, moderate detail, the same hand as the painted portrait references; soft painterly edges with a few crisp strokes. Each element has a strong, simple, readable shape that still reads when it is shrunk to 24 pixels. It is not a cartoon: no cel shading, no thick black outlines, no glossy 3D render, no vector shapes, no airbrushed gradients, no pixel art, no photograph.

Background: perfectly flat pure white (#FFFFFF), completely uniform from edge to edge, with no texture, no vignette, no grid lines, no frames, no labels, no numbers and no text. Every element stands apart from the others with clear background between them, and none touches the edge of the image.
