A game effects sheet: the inner core of a creature's fire breath, painted on black: four frames of the same stream of fire, one in each cell of a 2 by 2 grid, each exactly where its grey placeholder is and as large as it.

In every frame the stream leaves a creature's jaws at the small bright point at the left of the cell and pours to the right, along the ground, widening as it goes. Paint only the core: a fierce jet of white-gold and yellow fire, brightest and narrowest at the left, which loosens into tongues and flickers and fades toward the right end. The outer billows are a separate sheet: leave them out.
The four frames are one loop: the jet's tongues and flickers move from frame to frame while its length and width stay the same.

Camera: the game's battle camera, high above the field and looking down at about 40 degrees, the way a player looks down at miniatures on a table; orthographic, no perspective. Anything lying on the ground is foreshortened: a round shape on the ground appears as a flat ellipse about two thirds as tall as it is wide.

These sprites are light: in the game they are added onto the battle field, so anything that is not black glows and pure black disappears. Paint them as glowing fire on the black, never on a coloured or grey background; no ground, no figures, no scenery, no cast shadows.
Ember's colours: a pale golden-white to golden-yellow core (#FFE9A8 to #FFB347), an orange body (#FD6533) and deep red edges (#7A1E12) that fade into the black; no smoke.

Painting style: painterly oil on a wood panel, visible directional brushwork, moderate detail, the same hand as the painted portrait references; soft painterly edges with a few crisp strokes. Each element has a strong, simple, readable shape that still reads when it is shrunk to 24 pixels. It is not a cartoon: no cel shading, no thick black outlines, no glossy 3D render, no vector shapes, no airbrushed gradients, no pixel art, no photograph.

Background: perfectly flat pure black (#000000), completely uniform from edge to edge, with no texture, no vignette, no grid lines, no frames, no labels, no numbers and no text. Every element stands apart from the others with clear background between them, and none touches the edge of the image.
