A game effects sheet: the outer billows of a creature's fire breath, painted on a flat blue key: four frames of the same stream of fire, one in each cell of a 2 by 2 grid, each exactly where its grey placeholder is and as large as it.

In every frame the stream leaves a creature's jaws at the small bright point at the left of the cell and rolls to the right along the ground, widening as it goes, its lower edge touching the ground and its billows rising above it. Paint only the outer body of the stream: rolling billows of flame and smoke along its whole length, thicker and darker toward the right end, where it spreads and slows, and ends in rounded, thinning puffs of smoke well inside its cell: never a flat or straight-cut end. Along the middle of the stream, where the dark line of the placeholder runs, the billows thin out: the bright core is a separate sheet drawn over them.
The four frames are one loop: the billows roll forward from frame to frame while the stream's length and width stay the same.

Camera: the game's battle camera, high above the field and looking down at about 40 degrees, the way a player looks down at miniatures on a table; orthographic, no perspective. Anything lying on the ground is foreshortened: a round shape on the ground appears as a flat ellipse about two thirds as tall as it is wide.

The billows are matter seen in daylight, not light: rolling orange and deep red flame billows (#FD6533, #C4532C, #7A1E12) wrapped in dark charcoal and soot-brown smoke at their outer edges and tops, lit by the high sun from the upper left and by their own fire from inside; they must hold their colour drawn over a light grassland, so no pale washed-out haze.

Painting style: painterly oil on a wood panel, visible directional brushwork, moderate detail, the same hand as the painted portrait references; soft painterly edges with a few crisp strokes. Each element has a strong, simple, readable shape that still reads when it is shrunk to 24 pixels. It is not a cartoon: no cel shading, no thick black outlines, no glossy 3D render, no vector shapes, no airbrushed gradients, no pixel art, no photograph.

Background: perfectly flat pure blue (#0000FF), completely uniform from edge to edge, with no texture, no vignette, no grid lines, no frames, no labels, no numbers and no text. Every element stands apart from the others with clear background between them, and none touches the edge of the image.
No blue, cyan or violet anywhere in the painted elements: the blue is cut away by the game.
