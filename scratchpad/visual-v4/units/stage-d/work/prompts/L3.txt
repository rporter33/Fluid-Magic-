A game decal sheet: cracks and scars in the ground, painted on white, six separate marks, each exactly where its grey placeholder is and as large as it.

Top: a long thin crack in dry earth running across the whole width, jagged, with small side branches.
Second: a wider crack with dark depths and crumbling edges and a few branches, across the whole width.
Bottom left: split ground: cracks spreading out from a central point across a flat ellipse of earth, the middle darker where the ground heaved.
Right and bottom right: three root scars: holes of torn earth where roots burst out of the ground, dark soil, broken root stubs, clods thrown around, each a flat ellipse.
The marks use dark umber, soil brown, bark brown and charcoal.

Camera: the game's battle camera, high above the field and looking down at about 40 degrees, the way a player looks down at miniatures on a table; orthographic, no perspective. Anything lying on the ground is foreshortened: a round shape on the ground appears as a flat ellipse about two thirds as tall as it is wide.

These are flat marks on the ground (decals) that the game multiplies onto the battle field: pure white means untouched ground and every darker tone darkens the ground under it. Paint only the marks, as stains seen from above, with no light and shade of their own, no highlights brighter than the white, no cast shadows, no objects standing up.

Painting style: painterly oil on a wood panel, visible directional brushwork, moderate detail, the same hand as the painted portrait references; soft painterly edges with a few crisp strokes. Each element has a strong, simple, readable shape that still reads when it is shrunk to 20 pixels. It is not a cartoon: no cel shading, no thick black outlines, no glossy 3D render, no vector shapes, no airbrushed gradients, no pixel art, no photograph.

Background: perfectly flat pure white (#FFFFFF), completely uniform from edge to edge, with no texture, no vignette, no grid lines, no frames, no labels, no numbers and no text. Every element stands apart from the others with clear background between them, and none touches the edge of the image.
