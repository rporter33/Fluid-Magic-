Image 1 is the reference: a finished game effects sheet: six frames of one fireball exploding on the ground, painted on pure black, in a 3 by 2 grid, read left to right, top row then bottom row. Paint this sheet again, matching Image 1 as closely as you can: the top row (frames 1, 2 and 3) stays exactly the same, the same painting, colours, brushwork, sizes and places. Only the bottom row (frames 4, 5 and 6) is repainted, with fire and embers only and no smoke anywhere: no dark clouds, no grey or brown billows, no smoke columns or wisps, because the game adds its own smoke.

Frame 4 (bottom left): the dome of frame 3 tearing open into curling orange and deep red tongues of flame, licking outward and down, its middle dimmer; the same low, wide dome shape seen from high above, no more than a tenth wider than frame 3, centred in its cell with black all around it.

Frame 5 (bottom middle): a ring of low flames running along the ground around a dark, burnt middle; the ring is a flat ellipse about two thirds as tall as it is wide, seen from high above at 40 degrees; the middle is dark charred ground with a few glowing cracks, not smoke.

Frame 6 (bottom right): small glowing embers and flickers lying in a broken ring on the ground, the same flat ellipse as frame 5, fading; no smoke.

Image 2 is the approved painted portrait of the game's Fire Sprite: it shows how fire is painted in this game; keep that hand.

Ember's colours: a pale golden-white to golden-yellow core (#FFE9A8 to #FFB347), an orange body (#FD6533) and deep red edges (#7A1E12) that fade into the black.

Background: perfectly flat pure black (#000000), completely uniform from edge to edge, with no texture, no vignette, no grid lines, no frames, no labels, no numbers and no text. Every frame stands apart from the others with black between them, and none touches the edge of the image.
