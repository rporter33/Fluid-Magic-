How it is built, and the checks

  
The effects layer is new code beside the mockup's, driven by one data table (45-fx-looks.js, the future content/fx.ts: every timing, count and colour-grammar value, and each spell's fx record). One effects atlas (2048 x 1024); each spell in its own containers, its particles pre-warmed in ParticleContainers (normal for matter, added for Ember only, as the new colour grammar says), pooled sprites and rope meshes for the large shapes; the burst and the vines sorted with the figures; ground rings squashed to the camera's 0.643; additive light pools; a warm tint on the lit figures and nothing more (units carry no glow); pushed shadows; decals in the field under its cap; the docs/12 flash limiter; the OS reduced-motion setting. Above 2x the short form plays without its linger, and at most 3 burst or field effects play at once. No Graphics are redrawn per frame and every effect is a function of story time, so a seek, a replay or a screenshot draws the same frame. The effects sit under the standards and block glyphs, so a numbers layer above them would never be covered.
