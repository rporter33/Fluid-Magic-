### Rule changes (built; owner to confirm)

1. **Natural's unexplored interior haze 6% → 4%** (the approved value, as in Clear). The hem's 12% peak and the 40% desaturation stay, so the
   frontier line is as visible as before; the interior of unexplored land is about 2 L\* darker than in pass 3.
2. **No Far relief on unexplored land** (was 40% of it). Zoomed out (under 85 px a province), unexplored land lies flat while explored land
   stands up sculpted.
3. **The start view keeps the alerts ribbon off the land**: at 1280×720 it opens 134 px lower, with the model's north edge just under the
   ribbon and the capital in the lower third.
4. **Day chips may stand on the oak beside the model** with a hairline leader, when the model has no free spot near the waypoint (the phone at
   Fit).
5. **A wild's name may be tracked tighter or 1 px smaller, or dropped**, rather than cross its outline (the Still Mere is unnamed at Fit,
   84.8 px a province, at 1440×900; it was already unnamed at the least zoom).
6. **The cliff face carries buttresses and a skylit top** in Natural.
