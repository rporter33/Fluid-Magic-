# AI (M1)

Goal: an opponent that expands sensibly, defends its capital, researches, and attacks
when it has an edge. It does not need to be clever. It must never stall.

`runAi(state, nationId): Command[]` is called every 5 game days. It is stateless apart
from a small `aiMemory` object kept inside `NationState` (target province, last plan day).

## Priorities per call

1. **Economy**
   - Set tax Normal everywhere; High in the capital if gold < 50 and unrest < 20.
   - Buy PD up to level 5 in every province adjacent to an enemy or independent with
     strength > 20; level 10 in provinces adjacent to an enemy nation.
   - Build a Lab in the second-best province once gold > 500 and a Temple in every
     province with pop > 8000 when gold > 300 (one build at a time).
2. **Recruitment** (capital queue, keep it 1 to 2 items deep)
   - If the number of field commanders < 2 + provinces / 6: recruit a leader.
   - Keep a 2:1 ratio of melee to ranged by troop count; recruit in batches of 10 to 20.
   - Keep mages at 1 per 4 provinces, minimum 2. Recruit the adept tier when affordable.
   - Priests: 1 per 5 provinces.
   - Scouts: 1 at start, replace if lost.
   - Weighted by nation personality (`aggression`, `expansion`, `research`, `pillage`).
3. **Research**: pick the nation color; always on. Mages without armies get order Research.
4. **Expansion**: for each idle field army (commander with ≥ 15 troops), evaluate
   adjacent independent provinces by `score = (pop / 1000 + springs * 3 + wellspring * 10) /
   defenderStrength` and attack the best if `armyPower > 1.5 * defenderStrength`.
   `armyPower` = sum over troops of `(hp + att + def + prot) / 4 * (1 + 0.1 * hasRanged)`.
   Independent strength is known to the AI (no fog for AI in M1).
5. **Defense**: if an enemy army is adjacent to an owned province with power > local
   defense, pull the nearest army back or recruit PD. The capital always keeps a garrison
   of at least 20 troops after month 6.
6. **War**: when `aggression * ownPower > 1.2 * enemyPowerNearby` and a border enemy
   province is reachable, target it; prefer provinces with high pop, Wellsprings, or the
   enemy capital when its garrison is weak. Combine two armies if a single one is short.
7. **Pillage**: Red AI pillages enemy provinces it cannot hold (personality `pillage`).
8. **Rituals**: cast the cheapest useful ritual when mana ≥ 2x its cost: summons when at
   war, Fertile Season / Bounty when at peace, Burn the Fields at an enemy province
   adjacent to a war front.
9. **Wellsprings**: once an owned Wellspring exists and a priest is free, send the priest
   to claim it with an escort.
10. **Pretender**: a fighter chassis joins the main army after month 3; an Oracle
    researches; a Monolith casts rituals.

## Difficulty

- Easy: gold income x0.8, planning every 10 days, no War step until month 12.
- Normal: as above with no modifiers.

## Scripts

AI commanders use default scripts: mages cast their highest castable spell, with damage
spells first; priests Bless; leaders Hold.

## Required behavior tests

- On a Medium map, the AI owns ≥ 8 provinces by month 12 in ≥ 80% of 20 seeds.
- The AI never has a recruitment queue empty for more than 60 consecutive days while gold > 200.
- The AI never leaves its capital with 0 garrison after month 6.
